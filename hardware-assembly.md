# Hardware Assembly

Physical mounting and assembly of all components.

## Cube Orange Plus

- Mount centered in the boat
- **Arrow on case must point toward the bow** (front of boat)
- Use a vibration-damping mount if possible
- Keep away from high-current wiring (battery, ESC power leads)

## Here2 GPS

- Mount **high** — top of the boat, clear view of sky
- Antenna patch (the flat surface) faces **up**
- At least **10 cm** from ESCs, motors, and battery wires
- Confirm the mode switch on the Here2 is set to **CAN** (not UART)

## Thrusters (T500)

- Mount on the rear/transom, equally spaced from the center
- Cable glands or sealed pass-through for the wires
- Submerged in water when running (T500 can run dry for MAX 10 seconds)

## ESCs (Basic ESC 500)

- Dry compartment, with airflow if possible
- They get warm under load
- Secure them so vibration/heat doesn't loosen connections

## R9 SX Receiver

- Dry spot
- Both antennas are vertical, at 90° to each other
- Away from carbon fiber and metal

## RFD900x Telemetry

- Antenna vertical
- Away from ESCs and battery wires
- Mount high if possible

## Battery

- Secured with straps — must not shift during operation
- Easy to access for charging
- Fused at the battery terminal

## Waterproofing

- All electronics in a dry compartment
- Cable pass-throughs sealed (marine sealant or grommets)
- Battery compartment waterproof
